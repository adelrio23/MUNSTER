Program Capsule
Coached by Mike Niksic (1965–1985, 346-221-4) and Bob Shinkan (1986–2013), Munster Baseball reached the Indiana Class 4A State Championship in 2002 and the Final Four in 2001, 2006, and 2010. The program has produced All-State selections, MLB draft picks, and over 100 college baseball players.
